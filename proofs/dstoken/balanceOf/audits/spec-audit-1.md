## Adequacy review

The formal success precondition covers every 160-bit `WHO`, valid symbolic caller and contract addresses, arbitrary storage map, canonical `balanceOf(address)` calldata, and call value zero. Its postcondition requires `EVMC_SUCCESS`, a 32-byte result equal to KEVM's storage lookup at Solidity mapping slot `keccak256(pad32(WHO) ++ pad32(1))`, and preservation of the account storage.

The formal unsuccessful-call precondition covers the same symbolic address and storage domain and every nonzero 256-bit call value. Its postcondition requires `EVMC_REVERT`, empty output, and preservation of storage. These are precisely the two branches of the emitted call-value guard. The restriction to canonical ABI calldata is explicit in `SCOPE.md`; malformed or trailing calldata and other selectors are dispatcher behavior outside the source-level `balanceOf(address)` call contract.

The source declares `_supply` before `_balances`, and the bytecode independently witnesses base slot 1. The entry claim executes the exact runtime from PC 0 with a computed jump-destination table, empty stack and memory, and ABI calldata generated by the pinned semantics. The result is constrained rather than left free. The unmetered Byzantine execution model, direct call context, and partial-correctness reading are all explicit.

No source loop exists, so no loop circularity is required. The two entry claims are a path partition, not bounded examples: `CALLVALUE = 0` and every valid `CALL_VALUE =/= 0`.

## Summary faithfulness

`VERIFICATION-SUMMARIES` defines no mathematical summary function. It imports only the exact runtime pin. The output expression uses bundled KEVM functions: `#hashedLocation`, `#lookup`, and `#buf`. Their roles match the emitted mapping hash, `SLOAD` default-zero behavior, and one-word ABI encoding. There are therefore no candidate summary equations whose guards, overlap, recursion, or boundary cases could diverge from the intended result.
